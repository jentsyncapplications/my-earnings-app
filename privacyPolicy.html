Privacy Policy

App Name: MyEarnings

Effective Date: [Effective Date]

MyEarnings ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

MyEarnings is designed as a fully offline income tracking application. The App does not require account registration and does not collect personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

MyEarnings does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Bank or payment information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers

The App does not require user login or account creation.

2. User-Entered Financial Data

The App allows users to manually enter:

  • Income source
  • Amount earned
  • Amount spent
  • Automatically calculated savings
  • Reference notes
All information entered into the App is stored locally on your device only. The developer does not have access to this data.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally using secure Android device storage. The App:

  • Does not transmit data to external servers
  • Does not use cloud synchronization
  • Does not share data with third parties

If you uninstall the App, your locally stored data may be permanently deleted.

4. Third-Party Services

MyEarnings does not integrate:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics services
  • Tracking SDKs
  • External data processors

The App operates independently without third-party data collection tools.

5. Google Play Services

While MyEarnings itself does not collect personal data, the App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect basic anonymized usage data as part of its platform services.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally, data security depends on your device’s security settings. We recommend using device-level protection such as PIN, password, fingerprint, or face unlock.

The developer does not have remote access to user data.

7. Children’s Privacy

MyEarnings is intended for users aged 13 years and older.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13 years of age, you should not use this App.

8. User Control

You have full control over your data:

  • You may edit entries within the App at any time.
  • You may delete entries manually.
  • You may uninstall the App to remove all stored data.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by revising the effective date above.
